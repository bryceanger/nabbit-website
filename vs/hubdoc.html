The Key Difference: Hubdoc Is a Document Fetcher; Nabbit Is a Receipt Capture Tool

This is the most important distinction to understand before choosing.

Hubdoc was built primarily to fetch financial documents — bank statements, utility bills, invoices — automatically from online portals. It connects to your bank and pulls statements on your behalf. Its receipt capture (physical receipt OCR via mobile) is a secondary feature.

Nabbit was built specifically for receipt capture — photographing a paper receipt, extracting the data with AI, coding the expense, and syncing to your accounting software. Its core workflow is field-to-QuickBooks for businesses where receipts are generated in the field.

If your primary need is fetching bank statements and supplier invoices automatically, Hubdoc is stronger. If your primary need is capturing physical receipts from employees and syncing coded expenses to QuickBooks, Nabbit is stronger.

GST/HST and CRA Compliance — A Detailed Look

Both tools store documents in compliance with CRA's 6-year retention rule. The difference is in how they handle tax.

Nabbit reads the GST or HST amount directly from every receipt image and separates it from the base amount before syncing to QuickBooks. Input Tax Credits (ITCs) are tracked per transaction automatically. At GST/HST filing time, your coded expenses in QuickBooks already carry the correct tax amounts.

Hubdoc's document fetching workflow typically delivers raw PDFs and statements to QuickBooks or Xero, where the accountant still needs to code the tax amounts manually. For high-volume receipt capture scenarios, this creates work that Nabbit eliminates.

The Xero Factor

Hubdoc is now a native component of Xero. If you use Xero, Hubdoc is included in your subscription and deeply integrated. If you use QuickBooks Online Canada, Hubdoc is a separate cost and the integration is less native.

Nabbit was built for QuickBooks Online Canada. If QuickBooks is your accounting platform, Nabbit integrates more deeply.

Which Is Right for You?

Choose Nabbit if: You use QuickBooks Online Canada; your main problem is employees submitting receipts (field staff, contractors, team expenses); you want automatic GST/HST coding per receipt; and you need a team approval workflow before expenses hit QuickBooks.

Choose Hubdoc if: You use Xero (Hubdoc is included in your Xero subscription); your main need is fetching bank statements and supplier invoices automatically; or you are an accountant managing document collection across multiple clients.
