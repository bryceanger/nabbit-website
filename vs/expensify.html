The Core Difference: Designed for Canada vs. Adapted for Canada

Expensify was built for the US expense management market — corporate cards, travel reimbursement, per diem, and US tax rules (IRS compliance). Canada is a supported market, but Canadian-specific features like GST/HST coding, ITC tracking, and CRA audit documentation are not core to the product's design.

Nabbit was built in Canada for Canadian businesses. GST/HST is not a tax code you configure — it is a default that Nabbit handles automatically from day one. CRA compliance is not a checkbox; it is the archive design. QuickBooks Online Canada is not just "QuickBooks" — it is the specific Canadian version with CAD chart of accounts and province-specific HST rates.

For a small Canadian business, this difference shows up every quarter when you file your GST/HST return.

GST/HST and ITCs — Where the Gap Shows

Filing a GST/HST return requires your business to know exactly how much GST/HST you paid on each expense — so you can claim those amounts as Input Tax Credits (ITCs) and reduce your net remittance.

With Expensify, getting to this number typically means: export expenses → open a spreadsheet → filter by tax code → sum the GST/HST column → enter the total in your GST/HST return. If your staff didn't manually enter the tax amount when submitting an expense, the number is wrong.

With Nabbit, every receipt Nabbit reads has its GST or HST extracted automatically. The tax is coded separately in QuickBooks. When your accountant pulls the QuickBooks GST/HST report at quarter end, the numbers are already there — no spreadsheet, no manual correction. For a business with 50+ receipts per month, this saves two to four hours per quarter minimum.

When Expensify Makes More Sense

Expensify is a strong platform for businesses where the primary workflow is corporate card reconciliation and travel expense reports. If your team travels frequently, submits per diem claims, or needs automated integration with a corporate card, Expensify's travel and card features are more mature than Nabbit's. For medium and large businesses (50+ employees) with complex approval hierarchies, Expensify also has more workflow depth.

Pricing Context

Expensify charges in USD. For a Canadian business, this means your monthly cost fluctuates with the CAD/USD exchange rate — and at current rates (approximately 1.35–1.40 CAD per USD), US-priced tools cost 35–40% more than their listed price. Nabbit is priced in CAD. What you see is what you pay.

What Should You Check Before Choosing a Receipt Tool?

Whichever product you pick, test it against the things that cause trouble at GST/HST filing time and in a CRA audit:

  • Does it read GST/HST as its own amount, not just the receipt total?
  • Does it capture the supplier's GST/HST registration number? CRA expects it on receipts of $100 or more to support an Input Tax Credit.
  • Does it handle the provincial mix of HST, GST plus PST, and GST plus QST?
  • Does a person review each expense before it posts to your books?
  • Does it keep the original receipt image for the six years CRA requires?

How Can You Test Expensify and Nabbit With Your Own Receipts?

Submit one reimbursement the way your team really would, with a mix of paper receipts: a faded thermal receipt, a fuel receipt, a restaurant receipt with a tip, and a receipt that shows GST and PST separately. Compare what each tool read against the paper, and note how many fields you had to correct by hand. Then ask what the tool does with the corrected data, because the cost of a bad read is an ITC you cannot support later.
