Why Contractors Lose More ITCs Than Anyone Else

Trades work generates more receipts than almost any other business type — materials, fuel, tools, subcontractors — and most of those receipts come from suppliers, hardware stores, and fuel stations that print on thermal paper. Thermal paper fades within two to five years. CRA requires you to keep records for six years. That gap costs Canadian contractors thousands of dollars in denied Input Tax Credits every year.

The second problem is distance. When your crew is at a job site in Brampton and you're quoting in Mississauga, there's no reliable process for collecting their receipts. They pile up in trucks, get left on dashboards, or end up photographed in a group chat that no one can organize at year end.

How Does Nabbit Work for Contractors?

Nabbit is three steps: photograph the receipt, review the AI reading, and approve. The expense appears in QuickBooks automatically.

  1. Photograph at the counter. Your crew snaps a receipt on their phone using the Nabbit app. No internet required — receipts queue and sync when they reconnect.
  2. AI reads vendor, amount, and HST. Within 15 seconds Nabbit extracts the vendor name, date, total, and HST amount. GST and HST are separated from the base amount automatically, so your ITC claim is already calculated.
  3. Review and approve. You or your bookkeeper reviews expenses in the Nabbit dashboard, assigns the expense category (materials, fuel, subcontractor), and approves. The coded expense syncs to QuickBooks Online Canada — no double entry.

Is Nabbit CRA-Compliant for Contractor Records?

Yes. CRA accepts digital images of paper receipts as sufficient documentation for ITC claims, provided the image is legible and includes all required fields. Nabbit stores full-resolution images in a secure archive, indexed by date, vendor, and amount. Every receipt is retrievable in seconds — which matters when an auditor asks for every HST receipt from 2022.

For purchases of $100 or more — common for material purchases at Home Depot, Rona, or a local electrical supplier — CRA requires the vendor's HST registration number to appear on the receipt. Nabbit flags receipts where this field is missing so you can follow up before the expense is approved.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I capture receipts on a job site with no internet?
Yes. Nabbit queues receipts captured offline and syncs them automatically when you reconnect. Field crews can photograph receipts at the lumber yard or supplier and the data uploads when they're back in range.
Does Nabbit separate GST and HST automatically?
Yes. Nabbit reads the tax line from the receipt image and codes GST or HST separately from the base amount. The province is detected from the vendor location so the correct rate is applied automatically.
Can my bookkeeper see the receipts without logging into my phone?
Yes. Receipts captured on your phone appear in the shared Nabbit dashboard. Your bookkeeper or accountant can log in from any browser, review expenses, approve them, and sync to QuickBooks — without touching your device.
What if a receipt gets wet or torn on site?
Photograph it immediately. Nabbit reads partially damaged receipts and stores the full-resolution image. For CRA's 6-year retention window, the digital image is the record — not the paper original.
Does Nabbit work with QuickBooks for contractors?
Yes. Nabbit integrates with QuickBooks Online Canada. Receipts are synced as coded expenses with the correct expense category, vendor name, and HST amount — ready for your accountant at year end.
