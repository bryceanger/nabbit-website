How the Integration Works

  1. Capture the receipt. Snap a photo on your phone or forward an email receipt. Nabbit accepts camera photos, PDF attachments, and emailed receipts.
  2. AI reads the receipt. Within 10–15 seconds, Nabbit's AI extracts the vendor name, date, total amount, and GST/HST amount. The tax is separated from the base amount automatically.
  3. Review and approve. The expense appears in your Nabbit dashboard (or your employee submits it for your approval). Verify the coding, adjust the expense category if needed, and approve.
  4. Syncs to QuickBooks Online Canada. The coded expense — including the GST/HST amount — appears in QuickBooks. Your bookkeeper sees a clean, categorised expense. No double-entry.

Built for Canadian QuickBooks Online Users

Most expense tools were built for the US market and adapted for Canada. Nabbit was built in Canada for Canadian businesses — which shows in the details that matter at tax time.

GST/HST is automatic, not a setting. Every receipt Nabbit reads has its GST or HST extracted and coded separately. Input Tax Credits (ITCs) are tracked per transaction, so your QuickBooks GST/HST report is accurate at quarter end — without a spreadsheet reconciliation.

CAD by default. Every amount is in Canadian dollars. No currency conversion, no US defaults, no surprises on your QuickBooks reports.

CRA 6-year archive. Nabbit stores the original receipt image at full resolution alongside the coded expense. If CRA audits your 2024 returns in 2029, you can pull any receipt by date, vendor, or amount in seconds.

Province-specific HST rates. Ontario 13%, BC 5% GST + 7% PST, Alberta 5% GST. Nabbit applies the correct rate per receipt location — no manual tax code setup required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Nabbit work with QuickBooks Online Canada?
Yes. Nabbit integrates directly with QuickBooks Online Canada — the Canadian version with CAD chart of accounts, province-specific HST rates, and CRA-compliant tax codes. Not a generic US connector.
How long does it take to sync a receipt to QuickBooks?
Most receipts are read by Nabbit's AI and available to sync within 10–15 seconds of capture. Sync to QuickBooks Online is near-instant once the expense is approved.
Does Nabbit automatically detect GST and HST?
Yes. Nabbit reads the GST or HST amount directly from the receipt image, separates it from the base amount, and codes it to the correct tax line in QuickBooks. No manual tax code selection required.
Can multiple employees submit receipts that sync to one QuickBooks account?
Yes. Nabbit supports team workflows where field staff capture receipts on their phones, managers approve, and the bookkeeper sees clean coded expenses in QuickBooks — no double-entry.
Does Nabbit work with QuickBooks Desktop?
Nabbit is built for QuickBooks Online Canada. It does not currently integrate with QuickBooks Desktop.
Is Nabbit CRA-compliant for receipt storage?
Yes. Nabbit stores receipt images at full resolution in a secure archive for CRA's 6-year retention requirement. Every stored receipt is indexed by date, vendor, and amount for fast retrieval during audits.
How does Nabbit handle province-specific HST rates?
Nabbit applies the correct provincial HST/GST rate based on the vendor location and province. Ontario receipts get 13% HST; Alberta receipts get 5% GST — automatically, without manual setup.
What happens if Nabbit reads a receipt incorrectly?
Before an expense syncs to QuickBooks, you or your team reviews and approves it. If the AI misread a value, you correct it in the approval step. The corrected data syncs to QuickBooks — not the original OCR result.
