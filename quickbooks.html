What Gets Synced to QuickBooks

Vendor name

Matched to existing suppliers in your QBO account or created as new.

Amount & tax

Total amount with HST, GST, PST or QST broken out correctly. CRA-compliant.

Transaction date

The date on the receipt — not the date you uploaded it. Period accuracy preserved.

Nabbit suggests the right expense account based on the vendor. You confirm or change.

A link to the original receipt image stored in Google Drive or SharePoint — attached to the QBO transaction.

Card / payment method

Matches the last 4 digits to the right credit card account in QuickBooks automatically.

Common Questions

Does Nabbit work with QuickBooks Desktop?

Nabbit currently integrates with QuickBooks Online. If you're using QuickBooks Desktop, you can export an IIF file from Nabbit and import it into QBD manually. Full QBD sync is on the roadmap.

What QuickBooks plan do I need?

Any active QuickBooks Online subscription works — Simple Start, Essentials, Plus, or Advanced. Nabbit connects via the official Intuit API.

Will it create duplicates if I also enter receipts manually?

Only receipts approved in Nabbit get pushed to QuickBooks. If you're entering receipts both ways, you'll want to choose one — Nabbit or manual. Most customers switch fully to Nabbit and stop manual entry entirely.

Can it handle HST and GST correctly?

Yes. Nabbit reads the tax amount directly from the receipt and populates the tax field in QuickBooks with the correct type (HST, GST, PST, or QST) based on the province. CRA-compliant by default.

Is my QuickBooks data secure?

Nabbit connects via OAuth — we never see or store your QuickBooks password. The connection uses the same standard as major Canadian banks. You can disconnect at any time from Settings.
