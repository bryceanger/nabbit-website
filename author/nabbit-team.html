The Nabbit Team

The Nabbit Team writes the guides and product pages on nabbit.ca. We build a receipt capture app for Canadian businesses, so our subject is the paperwork behind a GST/HST return: receipts, Input Tax Credits, record keeping, and QuickBooks Online Canada.

Who Is the Nabbit Team?

Nabbit is a Canadian receipt capture product from 1001583085 Ontario Inc., based in Ontario. The team that builds the app also writes these pages, so the guidance comes from the people who work with the receipt problem every day: photographing receipts, reading vendor, date, total and GST/HST, and syncing approved expenses to QuickBooks Online Canada.

What Do We Write About?

Our guides cover what CRA requires on a receipt, how long to keep business records, how to track and claim GST/HST Input Tax Credits, how to categorize business expenses, and how Nabbit compares with other receipt tools. We also publish plain-language product pages for contractors and for QuickBooks Online users.

How Do We Check Our Work?

Each guide cites Canada Revenue Agency publications in a Sources section so you can read the rule at its source. Every page shows when it was last updated. Our guides are general information, not tax advice, so confirm anything that depends on your situation with your accountant. If you spot an error, email hello@nabbit.ca and we will correct it.
