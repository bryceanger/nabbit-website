The CRA rule is clear

Under the Excise Tax Act, you must hold sufficient documentary evidence — typically an original receipt — to claim an Input Tax Credit on a GST/HST purchase. If you’re audited and can’t produce the receipt, the ITC is denied. No exceptions.

A missing $48 restaurant receipt isn’t just $48 lost — it’s the HST on that $48, denied. Across dozens of receipts a month, that adds up fast.

Source: CRA — Input tax credits · Excise Tax Act, s. 169(4)
