What happens at each step

Step 1 — Snap or drop

Most receipts are lost within 24 hours of the purchase — tucked in a pocket, left in a vehicle, faded in a wallet. Nabbit is built to catch the receipt at the moment it exists: open the app on your phone, tap the camera, and the receipt is captured before you leave the parking lot. On desktop, drag a PDF from your Downloads folder or paste a screenshot. Nabbit handles paper receipts, digital PDFs, email attachments, and screenshots.

Step 2 — Nabbit reads it

Once captured, Nabbit's AI extracts the vendor name, transaction date, total amount, and HST/GST automatically. For receipts above the CRA's $30 threshold, it also pulls the supplier's GST/HST registration number when it appears on the receipt. You get a pre-filled expense card — vendor, date, amount, tax, suggested category — that you confirm or correct in a single tap. No typing required.

Nabbit learns your categorisation patterns over time. The first time you approve a Shell receipt as "Fuel & Vehicle," it remembers. The next Shell receipt goes straight to the right category.

Step 3 — Review queue for your bookkeeper

Every captured receipt sits in a Pending queue. Your bookkeeper or admin reviews each one — checking the GL account, adding a memo, confirming the HST amount — and approves individually or selects dozens at once for bulk approval. Nothing posts to your accounting software without a human sign-off. This keeps your books clean and gives your accountant confidence in every line item at year-end.

Step 4 — Sync and close

On Business and Team plans, approved expenses post automatically to QuickBooks Online — no CSV exports, no manual journal entries. The original receipt image is attached to the transaction, so your audit trail is complete. On the Personal plan, receipts land in a structured Google Drive folder with a running tracker spreadsheet your accountant can access at tax time.

Every receipt image is stored securely for the full six years the CRA requires — so even a 2026 receipt is retrievable if the CRA comes calling in 2031.

What about the receipts you missed?

Upload your credit card statement and Nabbit cross-references every line against what you've already captured. Anything missing gets a placeholder so no ITC falls through the cracks. If your QuickBooks bank feed already imported the same transaction, Nabbit matches them — no duplicates.
